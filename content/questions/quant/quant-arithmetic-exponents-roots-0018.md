## Question

What is the value of \((2^3)^2\)?

## Explanation

A **power raised to a power** means you **multiply** the exponents:

\[(2^3)^2 = 2^{3 \cdot 2} = 2^6 = 64\]

**The trap:** choice **A (32)** is \(2^5\), from *adding* the exponents (\(3 + 2\)) — that's the
rule for multiplying powers like \(2^3 \cdot 2^2\), not for a power of a power. Choice **B (512)**
is \(2^9\), from reading the expression as \(2^{(3^2)}\). Choice **D (8)** ignores the outer
exponent entirely (\(2^3\)), and **E (12)** multiplies the digits \(2 \cdot 3 \cdot 2\).

## Hints

- The whole quantity \(2^3\) is being squared.
- For \((a^m)^n\), do you add or multiply \(m\) and \(n\)?
